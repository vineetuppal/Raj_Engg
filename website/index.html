Precision engineering, since 2009.

2009

Raj Engineering Industries is expanding its wings across the nation by manufacturing, exporting and trading best-in-class tractor parts from Ludhiana, Punjab.

We are supported by a modern manufacturing unit equipped with all the requisite amenities, the backbone of our success. It lets us produce premium-quality spare parts and tools within the promised time frame, every time.

Our parts are made with high precision to global quality standards, using quality-assured raw material and modern technology. Clients know them for robust construction, excellent strength, rust resistance, easy handling and durability, available in customised options at genuine rates.

Under the direction of our proprietor, Mr. Rajinder Dhawan, we have satisfied clients year after year. He constantly motivates our team to give their best.
